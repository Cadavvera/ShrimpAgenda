Cherry Shrimp

These beautiful, red-colored shrimp will stand out and look great in your tank. These shrimp are Sakura Grade which are more translucent compared to the Fire Grade Cherry shrimp we carry. They are however, still a beautiful shrimp to have in any tank.

Blue Dream

Blue Dream Shrimp are one of the most popular blue Neocaridina varieties in the aquarium hobby, prized for their deep, opaque sapphire blue coloration.Their rich blue color, combined with the hardiness of all Neocaridina davidi varieties, makes them an excellent choice for both beginners and experienced shrimp keepers.

Orange Pumpkin

The Orange Pumpkin is an orange variant of the Cherry shrimp, sitting right in the middle of the colour spectrum between a Red Cherry and a Yellow Fire. It’s part of the same Neocaridina family of shrimp that originates from Taiwan.

Amano

If you keep a planted aquarium, you have almost certainly heard of Amano shrimp. Named after the late Takashi Amano — the Japanese aquascaper who single-handedly elevated freshwater aquascaping into an art form — these unassuming crustaceans are considered the gold standard for biological algae control in planted tanks worldwide.

Yellow Golden Back

The defining trait is a bright, solid golden or neon-yellow "racing stripe" running down its back, contrasting with its lighter yellow body. Lower lighting and a dark substrate can help them achieve the most vibrant colors .

Green Jade

Green Jade shrimp are an eye-catching Neocaridina variety that stand out with their deep emerald coloration. They add a natural, earthy tone to aquascapes and look especially striking against mosses, dark substrates, and wood. Their beauty comes from both pigment and structural color, giving them a glossy, layered look.

Bloody Mary

What makes them truly unique among red Neocaridina varieties is that their brilliant red coloration comes from pigment within their body tissue rather than from their shell. This creates a distinctive translucent appearance with the color appearing to glow from within, earning them their dramatic name.

Crystal Red

The crystal red shrimp is a stunning ornamental shrimp bred from the wild bee shrimp of southern China and Hong Kong. Through meticulous selective breeding, this variety displays bold alternating bands of opaque white and deep red on a translucent body.

Blue Jelly

A stunning freshwater shrimp variety known for their beautiful translucent sky-blue coloration and peaceful temperament. Their unique pastel blue appearance creates an elegant contrast against lush green aquatic plants and dark hardscape, making them a favorite for Nature Aquariums and planted tanks.

Orange Rili

Orange Rili shrimp are a type of Neocaridina with bright orange bodies and the signature Rili band around the middle of their body. They look very similar to an Orange Pumpkin shrimp but with an added Rili body band. Being part of the Neocaridina group, they prefer living in fairly hard water.

Snowball

Are a beautiful freshwater shrimp variety admired for their translucent white to pearl-colored bodies and their striking white eggs, which resemble tiny snowballs when females are berried. Their elegant appearance makes them an excellent choice for planted aquariums and peaceful community tanks.

Extreme Black

The Extreme Black King Kong Shrimp is one of the most striking and sought-after Caridina shrimp varieties, known for its deep, jet-black coloration that covers the entire body — from head to tail. These shrimp are selectively bred from Taiwan Bee genetics and are prized for their bold, uniform appearance and rarity in the hobby.
